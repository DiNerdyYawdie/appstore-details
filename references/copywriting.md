# App Store Copywriting Rules

## Description Structure
- First sentence: what the app does, for whom, and the core benefit
- Second and third sentences: the most compelling features
- These three lines show before the More tap — treat them like a headline
- Body paragraphs: expand on features, use cases, what makes it unique
- Final line: EULA

## Writing Style
- Write for the person browsing, not the developer
- Lead with benefits not features
- Use active voice
- Short sentences and short paragraphs
- Never use jargon the user would not understand
- Avoid superlatives like "best", "amazing", "incredible" unless substantiated
- Write in second person: "you", "your"

## Promotional Text
- One strong sentence or two short sentences
- Hook the reader immediately
- Use it to highlight the most recent or most compelling reason to download
- Update it seasonally or after major updates without needing a new submission

## Subtitle
- One clear value proposition
- Complement the app name, do not repeat it
- Make it scannable at a glance

## What Not To Write
- Do not mention price or pricing tiers in any field
- Do not reference other platforms or operating systems
- Do not include calls to action like Download Now or Get It Today
- Do not mention ratings or reviews
- Do not make claims you cannot substantiate
- Do not use ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Do not use excessive punctuation
