# Keyword Strategy for App Store ASO

## Core Principles
- 100 characters is your entire budget — spend every one
- No spaces after commas: word1,word2,word3 not word1, word2, word3
- Never duplicate words from your app name or subtitle
- Apple treats singular as both singular and plural — use singular form
- Short keywords get more impressions, long keywords get more targeted traffic

## Keyword Selection Process
1. Identify the core action your app performs (verbs)
2. Identify the category/genre
3. Identify the target audience
4. Identify related concepts and synonyms
5. Combine to fill as close to 100 characters as possible

## What To Include
- Core functionality words
- Category and genre terms
- Platform-specific terms if relevant (tv, television, couch, family, etc.)
- Synonyms for your main features
- Audience descriptors

## What To Exclude
- Words already in your app name
- Words already in your subtitle
- Apple trademark terms (iPhone, iPad, Apple, etc.)
- Competitor app names
- Irrelevant trending terms
- Spaces after commas (wastes characters)

## Localization Tip
Each locale gets its own 100 character keyword field.
Adding UK English, Canadian English, Australian English
triples your keyword surface area with minimal effort.
